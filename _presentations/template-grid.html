$$\theta^* = \arg\min_\theta \mathbb{E}_{(x,y)\sim\mathcal{D}}\big[\ell(f_\theta(x), y)\big] + \lambda \Omega(\theta)$$

  • \(\Omega(\theta)\) is the structural prior.
  • \(\lambda\) controls the prior–fit tradeoff.
